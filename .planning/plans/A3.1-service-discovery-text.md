## The decision, as it goes into the text

1. Choosing a chat server: a load balancer with an application-level health check
   (socket accepted, `hello` answered), random assignment. "Least-loaded" is dropped.
   `GET /chat-servers` is static or disappears behind the balancer's address.
2. Cleanup after a chat-server death: nobody runs `mark-all-offline`. Routing heals from
   the client (reconnect within 15 s by the mirror rule overwrites the route); a push to
   the dead node fails inside the bounded buffer's timeout and the message is already in
   the stream; presence is cleaned only by the lease (renew 2 min, TTL 5 min, 125 k
   writes/s, unchanged). The presence window on a node loss is up to 5 min, said plainly
   where §1.5 promised "within seconds".
3. Rejected: a registry (etcd / Consul / orchestrator) for least-loaded; a liveness
   watcher for the lost-server-and-network coincidence; a 30 s lease TTL (1.5 M writes/s).
4. Conditions carried into the text: app-level health check; bounded buffer timeout
   shorter than the N1 p99 budget (500 ms). §7.8's prototype default is 5 s, which is
   stated as a fact next to the condition, not resolved.
