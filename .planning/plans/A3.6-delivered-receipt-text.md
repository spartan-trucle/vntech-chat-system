## The decision, as it goes into the text

1. Delivered means the device stored the message in its local store.
2. Frame `delivered{conversation_id, up_to_message_id}`, client to server, in replay
   mode and live mode alike, after the store write, coalesced to at most one per
   conversation per 2 s (the read receipt's rule and number, §3.4).
3. Server: `SET last_delivered_id = max(last_delivered_id, up_to_message_id)` on
   `delivery_cursors` (§3.3); no per-connection state, no stream-id mapping. The
   push-to-socket advance is withdrawn (B5 finding 10 closed by removal).
4. `ack{up_to_stream_id}` unchanged: the sync cursor only (item 7). Different keys and
   owners: one Redis stream id per device, owned by the device; one Snowflake per
   conversation per device, owned by the server for F8.
5. Resend: the client keeps per conversation the highest `message_id` stored and the
   highest it sent a `delivered` for; after `replay_done` on a new connection it sends
   one `delivered` for every conversation where the two differ.
6. Derived status in §3.3 unchanged.
7. Rejected: push-to-socket advance; replay-ack-only (§7.3's proposal); ack extended to
   both modes with a server-side map; delivered on flush confirmation.
