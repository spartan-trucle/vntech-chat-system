## The decision, as it goes into the text

1. No primary. Cassandra, RF 3. The failure designed for is two of a token range's
   three replicas down at once. Cluster or region loss is deferred to spec §8.7.
2. Writes at LOCAL_QUORUM. On a QUORUM timeout the chat server retries once at ONE and
   acks. Budget 300 ms + 300 ms, flagged as the author's numbers to confirm. A write
   that fails even at ONE is not acked: outbox shows "sending…", resends on backoff.
3. N3 becomes "absolute at QUORUM; during a degraded range, once replicated". The
   write-order invariant (§4.2) is unchanged.
4. Dedup at two layers: the index read at LOCAL_QUORUM fails open on timeout (stores);
   the `deliver` frame carries `sender_id` and `client_msg_id` and the client drops a
   second message with the same pair.
5. Reads (page, summaries, cursors) follow the same rule, so normal mode is
   R + W = 4 > RF. A page served at ONE may miss a message that replay delivers.
6. Recovery: hinted handoff (3 h window) then repair. Operators page on the count of
   writes acked at ONE per minute.
7. Loss bound: zero normally. In a degraded window, the messages acked at ONE on that
   range if the last replica also dies before hints replay.
8. N2 statement: available through the loss of up to two replicas of any range. All
   three, or the cluster, holds sends in the outbox. Multi-region deferred to §8.7.
9. Rejected: ONE always (the author's first choice, revised), QUORUM without fallback,
   client-only dedup, refusing the send on a dedup timeout.
